How to play W&OD

    
Words & Omitted Details is a daily anagram game. You get one funny sentence with several blanks, and every missing word is an anagram of the others — the exact same letters, reordered.

    
The goal

    
Fill every blank using the fewest hints. You start at 10 points. Reveal a letter from the shared rack and you lose 1; submit a wrong answer and you lose 1. Fewer hints = a better score. Time is only a tiebreaker.
