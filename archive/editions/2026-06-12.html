Good Friday morning. It's a big weekend for getting outside — aircraft, a Cajun fest, a pollinator party, and patios full of music. Here's the plan.

Ashburn Weekend
Editor's pick

Sat 6/13 — Innovations in Flight Family Day at the Udvar-Hazy Center. 60+ aircraft fly in and park on the tarmac, 10am–3pm, with indoor kids' activities too. Free; reserve a timed pass for the outdoor display; $15 parking. Go early — the lot fills before the planes do. (Air & Space Museum)

This weekend
Sat 6/13 — Cajun Fest at Breaux Vineyards 11am–6pm: crawfish, zydeco, and a family-friendly vineyard day.
Sat 6/13 — Annual Pollinator Festival at Banshee Reeks Nature Preserve, 10am–2pm: hayrides, festival games, and hands-on nature exhibits. Free, all ages.
Sat 6/13 — "7 Bridges: The Ultimate Eagles Experience" at Tarara, 6pm. A grown-up evening if you can swing a sitter.
All weekend — the One Loudoun Carnival keeps spinning (through 6/21).
Out & About (grown-up picks)
