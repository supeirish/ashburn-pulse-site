Five days to the summer handoff. Here's the calendar, the camps and leagues still taking sign-ups, and a couple of civic things worth two minutes.

Ashburn Family

The big one

Last day of school is Monday, June 15. Expect early-dismissal schedules and report cards that week — check your school for exact times. Then the "I'm bored" era begins, so the rest of this section is your buffer. (LCPS)

Registration Radar — still open

  • County summer camps (Daze, K–5 / Fest, 6–8) are still enrolling — art, nature, culinary, STEM, dance and more, half- or full-day. Popular weeks sell out first; register on PRCS Connect, and financial assistance is available. (Parks & Rec)
  • Loudoun Soccer fall registration is open. U6–U8 rec divisions close first; financial-aid applications are due about two weeks before regular registration ends. (Loudoun Soccer)

First-week-of-summer wins

  • Rocknoceros on the One Loudoun Plaza, Mondays all summer (through 8/31). Free, all-ages, and it genuinely wears them out.
  • The Carnival at One Loudoun Uptown, through 6/21. Best for elementary-age; go on a weeknight to skip the weekend crush.
  • County pools are open — day passes are cheapest before noon on weekdays.

Ask Ashburn

"What's going in the old mattress store on Waxpool?" Per the county permit portal: a pediatric dental practice, targeting fall. (Got a question? Reply and we'll chase it down.)

Worth two minutes

The county's data-center Phase 2 rules head to Planning Commission public comment in July, and the county is fighting to keep an Amazon data center off the former GW campus in Ashburn. If you have opinions on noise, setbacks, and where centers go, July is when residents weigh in — we'll post the exact dates. (Loudoun County)

The Pulse

  • Opens today: Tatte Bakery & Cafe, 7 a.m. at One Loudoun. Quietest before 8 or after 10:30; Exchange St parking fills first. A good "bribe the kids" stop on a slow summer morning.
  • Opening Radar:

- Another Broken Egg — late-June target at Bram Quarter (kid-friendly brunch).

- Mystery tenant signed at a local shopping center — no permit filed yet, so a reveal is weeks out; we'll name it when it surfaces. (The Burn)

  • Splash-pad debate, settled: r/loudoun crowd-sourced the best spots for little kids — the One Loudoun fountains took the top seed, with shaded neighborhood playgrounds close behind. Free and stroller-friendly.

This weekend, quick look 👋 (full Weekend edition lands Friday)

  • NEW — Sat 6/13: Asia in a Bite Food Fest at One Loudoun — 40+ food vendors, a DJ, free and open to the public. Go hungry and early.
  • Plus the Saturday farmers market at One Loudoun (9am–1pm, free).
