Ashburn Weekend
Editor's pick

Fri 6/19 — Juneteenth at Ida Lee. County offices, courts, and libraries are closed (trash/recycling may shift a day). The Ida Lee Juneteenth Celebration in Leesburg runs noon–7pm — performers, food, and vendors, free. (Ida Lee Park)

This weekend
Sat 6/20 — Father's Day patio music. Brian Harris at Breaux (2pm) and Delta Spur (country) at Tarara (6pm). Tarara's Saturday Summer Concert Series is rolling all season.
Sat–Sun — Western Loudoun Art & Studio Tour: 40+ artists, self-driving and scenic — a slow Father's Day afternoon.
Last call — the Carnival at One Loudoun ends 6/21. Final weekend for the rides.
Eat & drink
Van Leeuwen Ice Cream is now open at One Loudoun — the post-carnival treat stop.
Tatte is open (quietest before 8 or after 10:30); Another Broken Egg still eyeing a late-June open at Bram Quarter.
Coming 6/27: One Loudoun's free Summer Concert Series kicks off on the Plaza.
