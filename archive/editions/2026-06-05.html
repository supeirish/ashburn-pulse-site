Here's your weekend, sorted — plus the openings worth tracking and one traffic heads-up before you head out.

Ashburn Weekend

Do this

Sat 6/6 — Polo in the Park, Morven Park. Real polo, picnic-friendly, and an easy afternoon even if you don't know a chukker from a mallet. Gates early afternoon; bring chairs.

This weekend

  • NEW — Ashburn Farmers Market, Sat 9am–1pm at One Loudoun. Produce, bakers, local makers. Free; leashed dogs welcome. Parking: Atwater garage is easiest. (Reader/organizer submission — verified hours.)
  • Sat 6/6 — Live music at Breaux Vineyards. Good first stop if you're doing a winery afternoon.
  • Through 6/21 — The Carnival at One Loudoun Uptown. Rides and games; best for elementary-age, and a solid weeknight option if weekends are packed.
  • Plan ahead, 6/13 — Tarara's "7 Bridges" Eagles tribute. These sell out; grab tickets now.
  • All month — Pride in the Vines winery passport. Collect stamps across inclusive Loudoun wineries.

Eat & drink

  • Tatte opens Wednesday 6/10, 7am at One Loudoun. If you want pastries without the opening-week wait, go before 8 or after 10:30; Exchange St parking fills first.
  • Juleps Kentucky Tavern is open in Ashburn — bourbon and a porch, well-timed for this weather.
  • Standing options: free line dancing Thursdays at Bluemont Station; Brews & Bingo at Lark, 1st & 3rd Mondays.

Rain plan

Mostly sunny, low 80s, with a slim chance of a Saturday-evening pop-up storm. If it hits: the carnival pauses for weather, so pivot indoors — One Loudoun shops, the library, or an early Tatte recon run.

Opening Radar

  • Another Broken Egg — coming soon, late-June target at Bram Quarter. Closest comp is First Watch; expect weekend waits.

The Pulse

  • Traffic: Lane closures on Waxpool Rd near Loudoun County Pkwy this weekend. Headed to One Loudoun Saturday? Take Gloucester Pkwy — Waxpool backs up past the Harris Teeter. (VDOT/511)
  • Ask Ashburn: "What's going in the old mattress store on Waxpool?" We checked the county permit portal — a pediatric dental practice filed to build it out, targeting fall.
  • Pools are open for the season, and county summer rec camps still have spots. Day passes are cheapest before noon on weekdays; popular half-day camps fill by mid-June. (Loudoun Parks & Rec)
