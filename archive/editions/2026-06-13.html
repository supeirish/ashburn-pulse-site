Good morning. Two things set the tone this weekend: school's out for summer (LCPS wrapped Friday 6/12 — they moved the end date up), and you've got one genuinely great outdoor day before the weather turns — sunny and ~87° today, then showers and storms roll in Sunday afternoon (80% chance). Ashburn Weekend
Editor's pick

Loudoun United is home tonight. The hometown pros host Rhode Island FC at Segra Field in Leesburg, 6 p.m. It's the marquee local sports night of the weekend — kids run the berm, real pro atmosphere, 15 minutes up the road. (Loudoun United)

Eat & drink — now open
Tatte Bakery & Cafe is open at One Loudoun (opened Wed 6/10). Go before 8 or after 10:30 to skip the line; Exchange St lot fills first. (One Loudoun)
Van Leeuwen is scooping at One Loudoun too — same row as Arhaus. A solid post-carnival cooldown.
Pinstack — the big new bowling-and-arcade complex — opened Friday (6/12). Bumper cars, laser tag, the works. Rainy-Sunday insurance for families.
For the calendar: Another Broken Egg Cafe opens Mon 6/22 at Bram Quarter — Loudoun's first. (Another Broken Egg)
This weekend
Sat–Sun 6/13–14 — One Loudoun Carnival, Uptown. Rides and carnival food, last week (through 6/21), free admission. Open Sat 1–11pm, Sun 3–11pm. Best for elementary age; go early before the heat. (One Loudoun)
Sat 6/13 — Polo in the Park, Morven Park. Real arena polo, two matches; gates 6 p.m., picnics welcome. A genuinely fun grown-up evening. (Morven Park)
Sat 6/13 — 7 Bridges (Eagles tribute) at Tarara, 6 p.m. (free), or Ozzy tribute (Ozzmosis) at Tally Ho, doors 7 p.m. — pick your decade. (Tally Ho)
Sat 6/13 — Loudoun Pours to Explore at Cana Vineyards, 6–8 p.m. Walk-around tasting from the Loudoun wineries association; June is the Pride in the Vines passport month. (Loudoun Wine)
Sun 6/14 — K-Pop Demon Hunters family dance party at Tally Ho (doors noon, show 1 p.m.) is sold out, but the farmers markets run rain-or-shine: Leesburg (Sat 8–noon), Brambleton & Cascades (Sun mornings). (Farmers Markets)
Kid-friendly pick

Sat 6/13 — Pollinator Festival at Banshee Reeks, 10 a.m.–2 p.m. Free, hands-on, hayrides — exactly the kind of morning-out that tires them out before the Saturday heat peaks. (Loudoun County)

The Pulse
Top story

Loudoun's leaders are signaling a turn on data centers. Amid the fight over Dominion's proposed transmission lines, county officials are publicly leaning away from more data-center growth — just as the Phase 2 data-center rules head toward Planning Commission public comment in July. That's the window residents get to weigh in on noise, setbacks, and where centers can go. We'll track it. (County: Phase 2 standards)

Quick hits
Free electronics recycling, Sat 6/13, 9 a.m.–3 p.m. at the Dulles Town Center lot behind Cheesecake Factory. Most items free; fees for TVs/monitors and hard-drive shredding (card only). (Loudoun County)
Early voting for the Aug. 4 primary starts Thu 6/18 at the Leesburg elections office (closed Fri 6/19 for Juneteenth). (Office of Elections)
Loudoun is eyeing a $356.6M bond referendum for the November ballot. (Loudoun Times-Mirror)
The new Dulles concourse is on track to open this fall — photos are out.
Development & roads
Algonkian Pkwy intersection work runs weeknights through 6/19 (intermittent lane closures at Hardwood Forest/Noble Terrace). (Loudoun County)
W&OD Trail tree work between Sterling and Ashburn continues through June — expect crews trailside.
City Tap House closed suddenly at One Loudoun; meanwhile signs are up at the coming Tous Les Jours bakery in Ashburn.

Tatte (One Loudoun) and Pinstack — now open (see Weekend).

Community note

The Loudoun County superintendent spent the week testifying on Capitol Hill, and the sheriff is asking parents to talk e-bike safety after a teen was hurt in a South Riding crash — a good reminder as kids hit the trails for summer.
