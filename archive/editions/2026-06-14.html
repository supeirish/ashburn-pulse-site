🏗️ Data Center Watch — part 1 of 3

A short series on the issue reshaping Ashburn, in plain English.

Homeowners in the Regency neighborhood (off Waxpool Rd and Ashburn Village Blvd) say they've been reportedly offered north of $4 million an acre to sell so developers can build up to six data centers there. It's the clearest sign yet that "Data Center Alley" is now knocking on residential streets. This connects directly to the county's Phase 2 data-center rules, which reach Planning Commission public comment in July — the moment residents can weigh in on noise, setbacks, and where centers are allowed. Next week (part 2): what it means for your power bill and your block. (NBC4 · County)
