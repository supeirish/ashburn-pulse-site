The fine print, in plain English

Privacy & Corrections

We keep this simple, and we say what we actually do. Questions? Email info@theashburnpulse.com.

Last updated: July 16, 2026

The Ashburn Pulse is a free, independent local newsletter and guide for Ashburn, Virginia, run by a neighbor — not a big platform. We collect as little as we can, we don't sell your information, and we're happy to delete it whenever you ask.

Privacy

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you subscribe to the newsletter.
  • How you found us, if you tell us (for example, choosing a referral source on a form) — so we know what's working.
  • Anything you send us — tips, corrections, listing submissions, or replies — including the email address you send them from.
  • Basic game info, if you choose to create an account for the daily puzzles and leaderboard: a display name and your scores/streaks. You can play without an account.

Who processes it

Our newsletter is sent through MailerLite, an email service provider that stores subscriber email addresses on our behalf. If you create a games account, your display name and scores are stored by our games back-end so the leaderboard can work. These providers process this information only so the Pulse can reach you and run the games — not for their own marketing.

Analytics & cookies

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how many people read the site and which sections are useful, and email opens/clicks are measured by MailerLite in the normal way for a newsletter. We don't use your reading to build advertising profiles, and there are no third-party ad trackers on the Pulse.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We do not share your email with advertisers.
  • We do not send you unrelated marketing — you get the Pulse, and you can leave any time.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time using the link at the bottom of any newsletter email — it takes effect immediately.
  • Delete your information. Email info@theashburnpulse.com and we'll remove your subscriber record and any games account, and confirm when it's done.
  • Ask us anything about what we hold about you — same address.
Contact. The Ashburn Pulse · Ashburn, Virginia · info@theashburnpulse.com

Corrections & accuracy

Getting the details right is the whole point of the Pulse. We check event details against the organizer's own website before we publish, and we hand-check the links we include.

When something changes or we get it wrong:

  • We update the web version of the affected page so it's correct going forward — we don't leave a wrong detail standing.
  • For a material correction (a wrong date, price, or location that could send you to the wrong place or time), we note what changed.
  • Spotted something off? Please tell us at info@theashburnpulse.com — corrections from neighbors make the Pulse better for everyone.
Events change on short notice. We do our best to keep listings current, but always give the organizer's own page a quick look before you head out — we link to it for exactly that reason.
