Family

If your student is new to LCPS this fall, the phone rule is the one to know.

The phone rule, for anyone new to it: LCPS Policy 8655 bans personal-device use bell to bell, aligning with new state law. It's been in force since January, so returning families already know the drill — but if you're new to the county or sending a first middle-schooler, it carries into the new year unchanged.
In force since Jan 5, 2026 · First day of school Mon, Aug 17

What Actually Matters

The power-line fight moves to the state's highest court, and there's a free walk-up job fair today.

The power line, where it stands: at a July 13 special meeting the School Board moved unanimously to challenge the state's Route 3A decision for the Golden to Mars transmission line — the SCC issued its final order June 29, and appeals go straight to the Virginia Supreme Court. The board also adopted a policy, again unanimously, banning future power lines on school property. The county tracks routes, case numbers and what's next on its transmission lines page. A joint meeting with the Board of Supervisors has been reported for tonight, though no official notice or venue has been posted — check the county calendar before you head out.
Reported for Wed, Jul 29, 6 p.m. · venue not posted
Walk up and hire — today: the county's Job Fair brings 20+ employers under one roof, with entry staggered across three one-hour slots and free paratransit available. Registration isn't required but does secure your slot.
Wed, Jul 29 · 10 a.m.–1 p.m. · Ashburn Rec & Community Center, 21105 Coopers Hawk Dr · Free
Worth knowing — the data-center squeeze: a 2022 county report found power constraints affecting roughly two-thirds of Loudoun's substations, with data-center growth outrunning Dominion's transmission buildout. Four years on it's still the backdrop to every power-line fight in the county, Golden to Mars included — treat the two-thirds figure as the last number the county put on paper, not a current reading.

The county's biggest rec-basketball window opens Saturday.

AYBL Winter Season — opens Saturday: Ashburn Youth Basketball League's winter registration runs Aug 1 through Sep 7, at $200 per player plus a $25 fundraising fee through the end of August (it goes to $225 after Aug 31).
