So many great options this weekend! The Leesburg Airshow is free and twenty minutes away, Oktoberfest is back in the Lovettsville streets for its thirtieth year, Temple Hall opens the pumpkin patch, and our own fire department runs a 5K that finishes before any of it starts. And there's plenty more, so check out this week's events!

The 5-second plan
Saturday, free, twenty minutes away: the Leesburg Airshow, where the parking is the part people get wrong.
Also Saturday, and it is the thirtieth: Lovettsville Oktoberfest, now in the downtown streets instead of the Town Green.
From Friday, in Ashburn: a circus at Loudoun Station. Tickets are $28.95 an adult from the circus, against about $83 on resale sites.
If you have a small child on Saturday: Cascades has cancelled its storytimes this week, but Rust, Brambleton and Ashburn are running theirs.
On the ballot: three county bond questions, and early voting is already open.

Editor's Pick

The Leesburg Airshow is free to get into, and you don't park at the airport. Free shuttles run from Segra Field and Heritage High School from 10:45. The Park and Ride on Gourley Transit Drive is the one lot with no shuttle, so it is worth avoiding. Flying starts at 1, and the lots fill up early.

Sat 9/26, 11 a.m.–4 p.m.; flying from 1 p.m. · Free, and the shuttles are free · ~20 min, Leesburg Executive Airport; park at 42095 Loudoun United Dr or 520 Evergreen Mills Rd · All ages, no pets

Saturday carries the most, and the 5K and the airshow stack neatly. Friday evening and Sunday afternoon are wide open.

Saturday
Lovettsville Oktoberfest turns 30 and moves into the streets. The streets around the Squirkle close to traffic at 6 a.m. Parking is free at Lovettsville Community Park with a shuttle running continuously from 9:30 to 6, and the town describes parking as limited, so it is worth arriving early. No admission price appears anywhere on the town's pages. If you see $25 on the event map, that is one of the parking lots rather than the gate.
Sat 9/26, 10 a.m.–5 p.m. · No admission figure published · ~35 min, park at 39550 Lovettsville Park Dr · All ages
The Ashburn fire department's 5K finishes before the airshow opens. The children's Spark Sprint goes at 9 and the 5K follows, so you can do this one and still make it to Leesburg. You can sign up on the morning, 7 to 8:30 at the brewery, or on Friday evening between 4 and 8.

What Actually Matters

Read the three bond figures before you vote. Monday at Broad Run is the one that costs you an evening.

Three bond questions are on your November ballot. Here is what each one borrows. The county's sample ballot asks for a maximum of $40,255,000 for schools, $35,431,000 for public safety and $280,848,000 for transportation, which comes to $356,534,000 added together. Early in-person voting is running now in Leesburg, and Ashburn's own site opens on October 18. Three constitutional amendment questions share the same ballot, so there will be six in front of you.
Weekdays now, 8:30 a.m.–5 p.m.; office closed Oct 12 · Free · ~20 min, 750 Miller Dr SE Suite 150, Leesburg · Registered Loudoun voters
The county takes comment on data centers at Broad Run High, and you can drop in. Staff are rewriting where data centers can go and what they must do about noise, height and power generated on site. There is no presentation to sit through, and county zoning enforcement will run noise-metering demonstrations. The hold is already in force: since September 15 the Board has been withholding final votes on data center and substation rezonings for up to a year. October 6 settles which filed projects keep the old rules, and October 20 formally enacts it.
Mon 9/28, 6–8:30 p.m.; repeats Wed 9/30 at the Douglass Community Center, Leesburg · Free · In Ashburn, 21670 Ashburn Road · Anyone, no sign-up

The Thanksgiving race is the one with money on the clock: $35 becomes $40 on Tuesday night.

Ashburn's Thanksgiving races cost $35 until Tuesday night, then $40. The 33rd running, out of Crossroads United Methodist, with the 10K at 8:15, the 5K at 8:25 and a 2K fun run at 8:35. There is a virtual option if you will be travelling.
Price rises after 11:59 p.m. Tue 9/29 · $35 plus a $3.10 fee now, $40 after · In Ashburn, 43454 Crossroads Dr · All ages
Dulles Youth Basketball evaluates every registered player Monday and Tuesday. Kindergarten goes at 6:15 Monday and 2nd grade at 7:00; 1st grade takes 6:15 Tuesday and "3rd Grade & Up" at 7:00. A child needs one of these sessions to be placed on a team, and after Tuesday night the next chance is the make-up on October 13. A refund requested now comes back less $75, and that window closes October 15.
Mon 9/28 and Tue 9/29 · $250 per child · In Ashburn, Cedar Lane Elementary, 43700 Tolamac Dr · Grades K–5, county residents
Waiting past October 2 costs $20 more a child at i9 Sports. Saturday basketball for ages 5 to 12 runs November 7 to December 5 at Virginia Academy, and the girls volleyball league for 7 to 14 shares the same Saturdays and the same cutoff. It is $169 if you pay by October 2 and $189 after that.
Pay by Fri 10/2 · $172 all in now, $189 registration after · In Ashburn, 19790 Ashburn Road · Basketball 5–12; volleyball girls 7–14

Save the Date

Waterford and the Farm Tour are the two October weekends people plan around.

Waterford Fair closes the village to cars for three days. Demonstrating artisans, living history through the lanes, and a homes tour with different houses open each day. Every ticket is an all-weekend pass this year. There are no ATMs on site, so bring cash, and make a note of your lot color.
Fri–Sun 10/2–10/4, 10 a.m.–5 p.m. · $20 advance plus fees, $25 at the gate; $20 seniors 65+, military and 13–21; free under 13 with an adult · ~30 min, village of Waterford · All ages, service dogs only
Brambleton throws its own Oktoberfest on the Town Center Green. Six hours on the green, about ten minutes away, if Lovettsville felt too far this week. No admission figure is published.
Sat 10/3, noon–6 p.m. · No admission figure published · ~10 min, 22890 Brambleton Plaza · All ages
Thirty-four Loudoun farms open their gates free, with no tickets. Self-guided, 10 to 4 both days, rain or shine. Printed farm lists are out at the Ashburn, Brambleton, Cascades, Gum Springs, Purcellville, Rust and Sterling libraries. Hogback Mountain Pony Rides is closed for 2026.
Sat–Sun 10/17–10/18, 10 a.m.–4 p.m. · Free; individual farms may charge · Countywide, with several stops near Ashburn · All ages
Two board votes decide how far the new data center rules reach. October 6 settles which already-filed projects keep the old rules. October 20 enacts the hold on final votes for data center and substation applications, backdated to September 15.
Tue 10/6 and Tue 10/20, 4 p.m. · Free, open meetings · ~20 min, 1 Harrison St SE, Leesburg · Open to the public
Early voting reaches Ashburn on October 18, with two deadlines behind it. The Ashburn Recreation and Community Center opens 11 to 4 that Sunday, and drop boxes open the same day at every Loudoun library apart from the Law Library. Register or request a mail ballot by October 23, and early voting ends October 31.
Sun 10/18, 11 a.m.–4 p.m. · Free · In Ashburn, 21105 Coopers Hawk Dr · Registered voters
