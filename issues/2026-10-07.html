Kids are off Monday, so the weekend runs three days. Olympic-level riders compete at Morven Park from Thursday to Sunday, Fairfax's Fall Festival turns 50 on Saturday, and Reston has a free rock show Saturday night. On Monday the libraries are closed, but the rec centers and the pumpkin farms are open.

The 5-second plan
Thursday to Sunday, about 20 minutes away: Morven Park International in Leesburg, free with a grounds pass.
Saturday, about 30 minutes away: Fairfax's 50th Fall Festival, free to walk in, with free shuttles.
Saturday night, about 20 minutes away: Rocktoberfest at Reston Town Center, free, with Vertical Horizon at 9.
Saturday and Sunday: fire station open houses, Sterling from 10 on Saturday and Arcola, ten minutes away, 1 to 4 on Sunday.
Monday, no school: the libraries are closed, but the pumpkin farms are open and Disney On Ice plays Fairfax at 1.
