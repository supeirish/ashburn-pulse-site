Editor's Pick

Brambleton Presbyterian's Family Giving Day packs 25,000 meals with Rise Against Hunger at The Barn at Brambleton on Sunday morning. It's open to all ages, and you sign up for the main shift or for setup or cleanup on either side. It's our pick because it's 10 minutes away, no charge is posted, and it gives a family two hours of real work together the Sunday before Thanksgiving.

Sun 11/22, 10 a.m.–noon; setup 8–10 a.m.; cleanup noon–12:30 p.m. · No charge posted; sign-up required · ~10 min, The Barn at Brambleton, 24439 Evergreen Mills Rd, Ashburn · Families with kids of any age
