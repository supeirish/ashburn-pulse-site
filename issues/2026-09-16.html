Editor's Pick

The Bluemont Fair is $10 at the gate, free for 9 and under, and it has been running for 56 years. A village of a few hundred people up against the Blue Ridge hands over its lanes for a weekend of craftspeople, music and food. There is no advance sale, so bring a card or cash, and go before noon: the lanes are narrow and the crowd thickens all afternoon.

Sat 9/19 and Sun 9/20, 10 a.m.-5 p.m. both days · $10 at the gate, free for 9 and under; parking free · ~35 min, 33846 Snickersville Tpk, Bluemont · All ages
This Weekend

Saturday is stacked and Sunday is quiet. The one Saturday night out is in Purcellville.

Fall Fest at Claude Moore Park is free, and the entrance is the part people get wrong. Park at the Loudoun Park Lane entrance by the Heritage Farm Museum. Rain or shine, per the county.
Sat 9/19, 10 a.m.-3 p.m. · Free in; crafts priced separately · ~15 min, 21668 Heritage Farm Ln, Sterling · Age range not posted
The Mills Pool throws a dog pool party Saturday, $10 a dog, no sign-up. People out at 3, dogs in at 4, benefiting Loudoun County Animal Services. The listing is written for Ashburn Village residents and says nothing either way about everyone else, so call ahead if you are outside.
Sat 9/19, 4-6 p.m. · $10 per dog; cash, check or card on site · In Ashburn, 43895 Grottoes Dr · Dogs and their people
The pumpkin fields open at Great Country Farms Saturday, and the same day ends with a flashlight corn maze. Separate tickets. Buying the day ticket ahead saves about $2 a head once the booking fee is in, and pumpkins are extra.
Day Sat 9/19, 9 a.m.-5 p.m., through 10/31; maze 6-8:30 p.m. · Day $14 child / $16 adult advance weekend plus a $1 ticketing fee, $18 at the gate age 3+, free under 2; maze $11 child / $13 adult online, $14 at the gate · ~30 min, Bluemont · All ages
The Reston Multicultural Festival runs seven hours, so go late morning or late afternoon and skip the crush. Its 25th year. The organizer publishes no admission price and does not say it is free either.
Sat 9/19, 11 a.m.-6 p.m. · No admission figure published · ~20 min, 11900 Market St, Reston · All ages
Last Ham Standing is the Saturday night out, $18 an adult and $15 for a senior, student or child. Long-form improv, and tickets are also sold at the door.
Sat 9/19, 8 p.m. · $18 adults, $15 seniors, students and children, plus fees · ~25 min, 36441 Blueridge View Ln, Purcellville · Adults and older kids
Reason to Rock is $20 ahead and $25 on the day, under 18s free. Live music for mental health programs. Check the address: this is Solace's Falls Church outpost, not the Sterling taproom.
Sat 9/19, from 1:30 p.m. · $20 advance, $25 day of; under 18 free · ~35 min, 444 W Broad St, Falls Church · All ages
Sportrock Sterling climbs for 24 straight hours, Saturday lunchtime to Sunday lunchtime. Teams of three rotate between climbing, belaying and resting. Members pay $15; the non-member price is not published.
Sat 9/19 11:30 a.m. to Sun 9/20 11:30 a.m. · $15 member entry; no non-member figure published · ~15 min, Sportrock Sterling · Teams of three
Penny-farthings, actually being ridden, outside Brambleton Library Sunday morning. Outdoors on the Willow Creek Way side, not inside the branch, which is where people go looking.
Sun 9/20, 10 a.m.-noon · No charge posted; just turn up · ~10 min, 22850 Brambleton Plaza · All ages
Lulu's Fate play Ashburn Library Sunday afternoon, 90 minutes, indoors, no ticket. Mandolin, cello and three-part harmony, listed from elementary up, so a sit-and-listen hour.
Sun 9/20, 2-3:30 p.m. · No charge posted; just turn up · In Ashburn, 43316 Hay Rd · Elementary and up
A fall makers market at Lark Brewing Sunday, free to walk into. Handmade and vintage vendors, taproom open alongside.
Sun 9/20, 11 a.m.-4:30 p.m. · Free admission · ~25 min, 24205 James Monroe Hwy, Aldie · All ages
The Brambleton farmers market is the calmer of the two EatLoco Sundays. Its own page calls it smaller and less crowded than Ashbrook, which is the honest reason to pick it with a stroller.
Sun 9/20, 9 a.m.-1 p.m. · No charge posted; just turn up · ~10 min, Olympia Dr, Brambleton · All ages

Mon 9/21, 10:30-11:15 a.m. · No charge posted; just turn up · ~15 min, 21030 Whitfield Pl · 18 months to 5, with an adult
A working illustrator teaches manga sketching at Ashburn Library Monday night. Follow-along format, so a beginner keeps up.
Mon 9/21, 7-8 p.m. · No charge posted; just turn up · In Ashburn, 43316 Hay Rd · Middle school and up

What Actually Matters

Early voting opens Friday. Thursday's noon cutoff is the one with a real cost attached.

Early in-person voting opens Friday, and for the next month Leesburg is the only place to do it. The four satellite sites, including the Ashburn Recreation and Community Center, do not open until Sunday October 18. No excuse needed.
Opens Fri 9/18, 8:30-5 · Free · ~15 min, 750 Miller Dr SE Suite 150, Leesburg · Registered voters
Out of the Darkness walks One Loudoun Saturday, free to join and free to register. Check-in 11, ceremony 12:30, walk 1, so plan a half day rather than a quick lap. A common source of teen service hours.
Sat 9/19, check-in 11 a.m., walk 1 p.m. · Free; free registration required · In Ashburn, One Loudoun · No minimum age posted
Ashburn Village exterior modification applications are due Thursday at noon, and an incomplete one will not be reviewed. Paint, fence, deck, shed, anything outside. Miss it and the project waits for the next cycle.
Deadline Thu 9/17, noon · No fee posted · In Ashburn, Ashburn Village Community Association · Ashburn Village homeowners
The Planning Commission hears the public Tuesday at 6, and sign-up to speak closes at noon that day. No agenda is posted yet, so sign up before noon and decide later.
Tue 9/22, 6 p.m.; sign-up closes noon · Free, open meeting · ~20 min, 1 Harrison St SE, Leesburg · Adults
Registration Radar

Western Loudoun has the nearest deadline, and the league says plainly it could come earlier than the date printed.

Western Loudoun Basketball is $200 a player and the October 1 close is tentative, not fixed. The league may move people to a wait-list earlier if divisions fill. Tryouts target 2 of 3 Saturdays on the calendar: September 26, October 3 and October 10.
Open now; tentative close Oct 1, possibly earlier · $200 per player · Western Loudoun · Grades 1-12
Dulles Youth Basketball posts $200 early and $250 after September 1, so today it is $250, and it closes when it fills rather than on a date. Card only, online only, and evaluations are mandatory: kindergarten Monday September 28 at 6:15 and second grade at 7, first grade Tuesday the 29th at 6:15 and third grade up at 7, make-ups October 13, all at Cedar Lane.
Open until capacity · $250 per child, card only · In Ashburn, Cedar Lane Elementary · Loudoun County residents, kindergarten and up
Central Loudoun Basketball has no deadline, because divisions close by filling. Middle school and grades 3 to 5 are already waitlisted; high school and grades 1 to 2 are open.
Open until each division fills · $200 elementary and middle, $180 high school, county fee included · Central Loudoun · Grades 1-12

Save the Date

Temple Hall opens the weekend after next, and the Morven Park grounds pass costs nothing but you still have to claim one.

Temple Hall's pumpkin and flower fields open Saturday the 26th, and the $15 already includes a pumpkin and a bouquet. No online sale, so pay on arrival, card preferred.
Opens Sat 9/26, 9 a.m. · $15 per person, $12 weekdays, free under 2 · ~20 min, 15855 Limestone School Rd, Leesburg · All ages
The JK Black Oak Wildlife Sanctuary opens to the public on the 27th, free, and it is normally closed. Registration is requested ahead, a guided walk starts at 11, and you park at the field on Stumptown and Newvalley Church Roads.
Sun 9/27, from 10 a.m. · Free; free registration requested · ~25 min, Lucketts · All ages
Lovettsville Oktoberfest returns to the downtown streets on Saturday the 26th, and the location has changed. If you went last year, the Town Green is not where it is. The town publishes no admission price.
Sat 9/26, 10 a.m.-5 p.m. · No admission figure published · ~35 min, downtown Lovettsville · All ages
Breaux Vineyards runs its harvest festival October 24 and 25, free to attend. The winery's own music lineup names both days, which settles a date that reads two ways elsewhere on the page.
Sat 10/24 and Sun 10/25, from 11 a.m. · Free to attend · ~30 min, 36888 Breaux Vineyards Ln, Purcellville · All ages
The Leesburg Kiwanis Halloween parade turns 70, and it falls on a Wednesday school night. Street closures downtown start at 5:45.
Wed 10/28, 6:30 p.m. · No admission figure published · ~20 min, downtown Leesburg · All ages
