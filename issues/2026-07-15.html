Editor's Pick

The Purcellville Wine & Food Festival is back — Sat 7/18. Its 13th year runs 2–8 p.m. at Fireman's Field in Purcellville: area wineries and breweries, local food vendors, and live sets from the Rick Reeves Band and the Franklin Park Big Band. It's $5 in advance, $12 at the gate (drink tickets sold separately). About a 30-minute drive west — make an afternoon of it and stop at a farm stand on the way home.

This Weekend

Our best bets. The full slate's down in Extra Credit.

The New Orleans party — Sat 7/18: Trombone Shorty & Orleans Avenue with Mavis Staples and The War & Treaty, 7 p.m. at Wolf Trap, Vienna. The biggest bill of the weekend if you're up for the drive (~40 min).
Best indie-funk night — Sat 7/18: Magic City Hippies with Doc Robinson at Tally Ho, Leesburg — doors 7 p.m., about 20 minutes out.
The only pro home game — Sat 7/18: Loudoun United FC hosts Tampa Bay, 7 p.m. at Segra Field (Nats, Spirit and DC United are all away). Budget-friendly pro soccer close to home.
Best Sunday wander — Sun 7/19: Locally Sourced Sip & Shop at Stone Tower, Leesburg — a local makers' market with wine by the glass and no entry fee.
Easiest Friday out — Fri 7/17: free live music (California Bob) 6–9 p.m. at Solace Brewing, Sterling — and Solace circles back Sunday with music bingo and tabletop D&D (2–3:30 p.m.).
Family

The easy yes — Thu 7/16: Lemonade Stand LIVE with Mr. Rob & Band, 10:30 a.m. at Bles Park, Ashburn — free; register ahead for lemonade and the first 100 kids get a bucket hat.

What Actually Matters

The one to act on: the power-line vote has a hard July 20 deadline.

Most important deadline — by July 20: the Golden-to-Mars power line. After the state approved Route 3A, the county has pressed the School Board to act on the easement by July 20 — the call that sets which neighborhoods the towers cross. If you live near that corridor, this is the one to watch.
Register to vote — by Fri 7/24: want a say in the Aug 4 primary? July 24 at 5 p.m. is the hard cutoff to register or update your address; early in-person voting is already open in Leesburg.
Registration Radar

Fall sports sign up over the summer — the deadlines worth putting on the fridge.

Loudoun Soccer — Fall Rec & Mini: registration ends Aug 8 — register online now.
Dulles Youth Basketball (grades K–5): open now — $200 through July, $225 in August, then $250 after Sept 1, so earlier is cheaper; Oct evaluations required. Register here.
Loudoun Youth Volleyball (grades 3–12): open now, $275 (early-bird ended Jul 10). loudounyouthvolleyball.com.
PRCS Fall Activity Guide: posts online mid-July — fall youth-sports windows open through PRCS Connect.
Loudoun South Little League — fall ball: registration open, T-Ball through Juniors (lsll.org); season runs late Aug–Oct.
Save the Date
The county fair turns 90 — Tue 7/21–Sat 7/25: carnival rides, livestock, the Pig Scramble and Cow Pie Bingo at the Loudoun County Fair, Leesburg. Ninety years in, it's still the closest thing we've got to a hometown week.
Ashburn Village Kids Triathlon — Sat 8/8: swim-bike-run for ages 6–15 at the Lakes Rec Center right here in the Village; $55 residents / $60 non-residents. Registration's open now and these fill up — sign up here.
Peach Fuzztival — Aug 1–2: peach pie, slushies and ice cream plus local vineyard and brewery partners at Great Country Farms, Bluemont.
Loudoun Wildlife Butterfly Count — early August: the annual community count is open for sign-ups now, beginners paired with experienced counters. (The Willowsford butterfly walk is already full, but the countywide count is the real thing to join.)
