Editor's Pick

Corrections
Setting the record straight
In the Jul 22 edition

We said: That the next milestone on the Golden-to-Mars power line was a joint public meeting on July 29. We wrote it flat, as settled fact.

What’s right: That meeting does not appear in the county's meeting archive or the School Board's, and we can't find that it happened. What did happen is that the State Corporation Commission closed the case on July 20, nine days earlier, denying Loudoun County's motion to suspend — which took the wind out of the meeting's stated purpose. The case (PUR-2025-00056) is now under appeal, with notices filed by the School Board, the county, two homeowner groups and two residents between July 17 and 28. The week after we ran it, we quietly softened the line instead of correcting it. Quietly is the wrong way to do that.
