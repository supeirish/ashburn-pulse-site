Today: Veterans Day

Leesburg's free program at Dodona Manor starts at 10, county offices and libraries are closed while schools stay open, and Meadowlark's light walk opens tonight.

Leesburg's free Veterans Day celebration at Dodona Manor starts at 10 this morning, rain or shine. Retired Lt. Gen. Bruce T. Crawford speaks at 11, and the barbecue lunch at noon runs while supplies last.
Wed 11/11, 10 a.m.–1 p.m.; tours and vintage vehicles 10–11; speakers 11–noon; lunch noon–1; park in the county garage on Loudoun Street by 9:30 for the Cartwheels shuttle · Free; RSVP link on the page · ~20 min, Dodona Manor, Leesburg (the Marshall Center lists 312 E Market St) · All ages

Editor's Pick

Saturday's LoCo Cider Fest at Great Country Farms pours cider and mead for the grown-ups while the kids get the run of the farm. An adult ticket covers eight tastings, a souvenir glass and the farm, with Loudoun Cider House and Mount Defiance among the pours, and kids get a non-alcoholic drink. It's our pick because it works as well for a family as for a group of friends, and $112 ahead for two adults and two kids is fair for a full afternoon in Bluemont. Buying ahead saves $5 an adult.

Sat 11/14, noon–5 p.m. · Adults $40 advance, $45 at the gate; kids $15 plus a $1 fee in advance, $18 at the gate (the page doesn't give kids' ages or say whether toddlers need a ticket); two adults and two kids $112 in advance, $126 at the gate, before taxes · ~35–40 min, Great Country Farms, 18780 Foggy Bottom Rd, Bluemont · Adults; kids welcome on the farm
