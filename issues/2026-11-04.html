The farms close for the season Sunday, students are off Monday for Diwali, and Veterans Day is next Wednesday.

The 5-second plan
Friday to Sunday, about 30 minutes away: big N-scale model railroads at the Purcellville library, with no charge posted.
Next Wednesday at 10, about 20 minutes away: Leesburg's free Veterans Day celebration at Dodona Manor, with house tours, a keynote and a barbecue lunch.
This Thursday, November 5, at 6:30 a.m., online: county winter registration opens for programs in the winter guide, which covers December 1 to February 28.
Sunday at 4, in Ashburn: the Loudoun Symphony Youth Orchestra's fall concert at Stone Bridge, expected to sell out; buy tickets ahead, since none are sold at the door.
Monday, the Diwali day off, in Ashburn: a $15 public skate at Ashburn Ice House at 11:40, after Playground on Ice for ages 2 to 9 at 10:30.

Editor's Pick

Local hobbyists fill the Purcellville library with big, connected N-scale model railroads, Friday to Sunday. The layouts take over the storytime area, and no sign-up or charge is listed. It runs 9:30 to 3 all three days, so it fits around a busy weekend.

Fri 11/6, Sat 11/7 and Sun 11/8, 9:30 a.m.–3 p.m. · No charge posted · ~30 min, Purcellville Library, 220 E Main St, Purcellville · All ages

Veterans Day, Wed 11/11

Leesburg's Veterans Day program at Dodona Manor is free with a barbecue lunch, libraries and county offices close, schools stay open, and Meadowlark's light walk opens that night.

Leesburg's Veterans Day celebration at Dodona Manor is free, with house tours, vintage military vehicles and a barbecue lunch. Retired Lt. Gen. Bruce T. Crawford speaks at 11; organizers suggest parking in the county garage on Loudoun Street by 9:30 and taking the shuttle.
Wed 11/11, 10 a.m.–1 p.m.; tours 10–11; speakers 11–noon; lunch noon–1, while supplies last; rain or shine; Cartwheels rides from the garage · Free; RSVP on the page · ~20 min, Dodona Manor, Leesburg (the Marshall Center lists 312 E Market St) · All ages
Loudoun libraries and county offices close Wednesday, November 11, for Veterans Day, and LCPS schools stay open. The library's notice points to its online resources for the day.
Wed 11/11, all day · Countywide · Anyone planning a library or county-office errand

Opens Wed 11/11; Nov 11–Dec 3, 5:30–10 p.m., last admission 9:30 · $19 online, $24 at the door, plus tax and fees; no child price posted · ~30 min, 9750 Meadowlark Gardens Ct, Vienna · All ages

What Actually Matters

The county takes input on housing and community needs next Tuesday, in Leesburg or online.

Tell the county which housing and community needs to fund at a hearing Tuesday, November 10, in Leesburg or online. The Housing Advisory Board takes input on housing, homelessness, community development and public services; online details are at loudoun.gov/CDBG.
Tue 11/10, 4–5:30 p.m. · Free · ~20 min, Olmstead Room, first floor, 106 Catoctin Circle SE, Leesburg; or online · Residents who follow housing and local services
