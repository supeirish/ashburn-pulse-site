Editor's Pick

The Loudoun County Fair rides out its final days — thru Sat 7/25. The county's biggest once-a-year draw is at the fairgrounds in Leesburg through Saturday: 4-H livestock, a carnival midway, bull riding and a mini demolition derby. It's $13.50 for adults and $5.75 for kids online, and about a 15-minute drive. If you've been meaning to go all week, this is the weekend — it won't be back until next summer.

Family

The easy yes — all weekend: slides, a lily-pad walk and zero-depth entry at Volcano Island, Algonkian — open Fri–Sun 11 a.m.–7 p.m. (to 6 the rest of the week); $16, $12 under 48". Works from toddlers to tweens.

What Actually Matters

The one to act on: Friday is the hard voter-registration cutoff.

Register to vote — by Fri 7/24: Friday is the last day to register for a regular ballot in the Aug 4 primary. Early in-person voting runs through Aug 1, including weekend hours at the Ashburn Recreation Center.
The power line, where it stands — updated: after the state's SCC approved Route 3A for the Golden-to-Mars line at the end of June, the Loudoun County School Board voted July 13 to appeal that decision to the Virginia Supreme Court and to ban power lines on school property. So the route is approved but now under appeal; the county lays out the timeline on its transmission lines page, and there's a joint public meeting on the route July 29.

Save the Date
Ashburn Village Kids Triathlon — Sat 8/8: a swim-bike-run for ages 6–15 at the Lakes Rec Center right in the Village; $55 / $60. These fill up — sign up now.
Peach Fuzztival — Aug 1–2: peach-picking, peach treats, contests and live music at Great Country Farms, Bluemont — a beloved annual farm weekend.
Loudoun Wildlife 30th Butterfly Count — Sat 8/1: the 30th annual community count is open for sign-ups now, all ages and experience levels, beginners paired with experienced counters (register).
Asia In a Bite Food Festival — Aug 29–30: a two-day Asian food festival with 40+ vendors and a live DJ at One Loudoun — a big in-Ashburn food weekend to put on the calendar.
