This is an extra-long edition, because this weekend is packed with fairs and festivals, so they get their own section below. Waterford opens its village for three days, Brambleton's Oktoberfest is free to walk into and ten minutes from home, and the State Fair of Virginia is in its last days. Closer to home, Venardos Circus plays its last shows in Ashburn.

The 5-second plan
Friday to Sunday, about 30 minutes away: the Waterford Fair, where one ticket gets you in all three days.
Through Sunday, in Ashburn: Venardos Circus's last shows at Loudoun Station, animal-free, from $19.95 for kids.
Saturday, ten minutes away: BRAMtoberfest is free to walk into, kids' ride bracelets cost less online, and there's a 21-and-over beer and wine garden.
Saturday, about 20 minutes away: downtown Leesburg has up to 150 show cars by day and yacht rock at Tally Ho at night.
Pumpkin patch this weekend? Pumpkin Village and Cox Farms sell weekend tickets online only, so buy before you drive.

Editor's Pick

One Waterford Fair ticket gets you in all three days, and the homes on tour change each day. The 82nd fair brings juried artisans demonstrating their crafts, traditional music and local beer and wine, and children can follow a cryptogram trail around the village to earn badges. Parking is free in the meadow lots, with shuttles running all day. There are no ATMs, so bring cash.

Fri–Sun 10/2–10/4, 10 a.m.–5 p.m. · $20 advance plus fees, $25 at the gate; $20 seniors 65+, military and ages 13–21, advance or gate; free 12 and under with a paid adult · ~30 min, village of Waterford, 40222 Fairfax St · All ages, service dogs only
Fairs, Festivals & Fall

Saturday alone has BRAMtoberfest, Vienna's Oktoberfest and Douglass Legacy Day, and the state fair's last days run through Sunday.

Fairs and festivals
BRAMtoberfest is free to get into, and kids' ride bracelets are $20 online, $25 at the gate. The bracelet covers every ride, as often as they like, from 1 to 6 for ages 3 to 16. Food trucks and live music fill the Town Center, and the Bear Chase beer and wine garden is $35 for a wristband and eight drink tickets. Park in the garage.
Sat 10/3, noon–6 p.m.; rides 1–6 p.m.; rain date Sun 10/4 · Free entry; ride bracelet $20 online plus a $1.58 fee, $25 at the gate; beer and wine garden $35 plus fees (1–5:30 p.m.), VIP hour $65 (noon–1) · ~10 min, 22890 Brambleton Plaza · All ages; beer garden 21+
Take a free shuttle or ride the W&OD to Vienna's Oktoberfest. Entry is free, and there are no festival parking lots. Expect three music stages, a free kids' zone on the Town Green and a global food court. Shuttles leave from Vienna Metro North, James Madison High School and Ayr Hill and Dominion, and there's a bike-and-park stop where the trail meets Church Street.
Sat 10/3, 11 a.m.–7 p.m.; shuttles 10:30 a.m.–7:30 p.m. · Free entry; drinks $8 a ticket or four for $30 · ~30 min, 131 Church St NE, Vienna · All ages; organizers ask you to leave dogs at home
Douglass Legacy Day fills the historic Douglass High School campus with food trucks, music and more than 50 vendors. Douglass was Loudoun's first and only Black high school, from 1941 to 1968. Kids get face painting, magic and animal shows and lawn games.
Sat 10/3, noon–5 p.m. · Free · ~20 min, 407 E Market St, Leesburg · All ages
Loudoun United's Oktoberfest night has $4 domestic beer and towels for the first 500. Indy Eleven visit Segra Field. A Kids Zone has inflatables and face painting, and children 5 to 14 can join the pre-match high-five tunnel.
Sat 10/3, gates 5 p.m., kickoff 6 p.m. · Ticket prices not posted on the club's page · ~20 min, Segra Field, 42095 Loudoun United Dr, Leesburg · All ages
Greek food, pastries and live music at St. Katherine's for three days, free to get in and park. Everything on the menu is available to carry out.
Fri 10/2 and Sat 10/3, 11 a.m.–9 p.m.; Sun 10/4, noon–8 p.m. · Free admission and parking · ~35 min, 3149 Glen Carlyn Rd, Falls Church · All ages
The State Fair of Virginia is in its last days at Meadow Event Park. Organizers announced a Sept. 25 to Oct. 4 run this year, so Sunday is the last day. Rides open at 11, the buildings close at 9, and the gate stops selling tickets at 8 p.m. General parking is free.
Thu 10/1–Sun 10/4, 10 a.m.–10 p.m.; rides from 11 a.m. · Gate prices not posted; parking free · ~1 hr 45 min, Meadow Event Park, 13191 Dawn Blvd, Doswell · All ages
Haunts and Halloween
Pumpkins and ghouls are hidden around Hanson Park from Saturday. Pick up a hunt sheet at the Nature Center and go looking, any day but Sunday until November 4.
Mon–Sat, 7 a.m.–7 p.m., Sat 10/3–Wed 11/4 · Free · ~20 min, 22831 Hanson Park Dr, Aldie · No age limit posted
Fields of Fear tickets are online only, $45 this Friday and Saturday. Cox Farms after dark: one run each through the Dark Side Hayride, the Cornightmare corn maze and The Forest. If you want an earlier entry time, the farm suggests arriving by 7. It isn't recommended under 12, and anyone under 14 needs an adult.
Fri 10/2 and Sat 10/3, 7:30–11 p.m., last entry 10 · $45 both nights; under 2 free · ~30 min, 15621 Braddock Rd, Centreville · Teens and adults
Milltown Haunt opens for the season on Friday, $40 a ticket. Cash at the booth avoids the online service fee, but Fridays and Saturdays are the busiest nights, so buying ahead guarantees your date. A $5 midway-only ticket skips the haunted attractions.
Fri 10/2–Sat 11/7; Fri–Sat 7–11 p.m., Sun 7–10 p.m.; gates 6 p.m. · $40 general, $55 skip-the-line, $5 midway only; online adds a service fee · ~35 min, 13936 Milltown Rd, Lovettsville · Teens and adults; under 16 with an adult
Pumpkins and corn mazes
Pumpkin Village and Cox Farms sell weekend tickets online only, so buy before you drive. Pumpkin Village charges $14.95 on Wednesdays and $24.95 on weekends, and Cox Farms prices each date separately and warns that busy days sell out. Temple Hall and Great Country Farms still sell at the gate.
Pumpkin Village daily except Tue, 9:30 a.m.–4 p.m. Mon–Thu, to 6 p.m. Fri–Sun; Cox Farms Thu–Mon, 10 a.m.–6 p.m. · Pumpkin Village $24.95 weekends, $19.95 weekdays, $14.95 Wednesdays; Cox Farms $15–$30 by date, $30 this Saturday and Sunday; both free under 2 · ~20 min, 19246 James Monroe Hwy, Leesburg; ~30 min, 15621 Braddock Rd, Centreville · All ages
Grown-ups get the Bluemont corn maze after dark from Friday, with a pour of cider or wine. The $45 ticket covers the maze, one pour of Henway Hard Cider or Bluemont Vineyard wine, live music and the fire pits. Tickets are non-refundable, and the farm says the nights sell out.
Fri–Sat 10/2–10/24, 6–9 p.m. · $45 a person · ~40 min, 18780 Foggy Bottom Rd, Bluemont · Adults only
This Weekend

Saturday centers on downtown Leesburg, with the car show by day and yacht rock at night, and Sunday belongs to the Ashburn fire station.

Friday
A page of George Washington's handwritten will goes on show in Leesburg for two hours. The Loudoun and Fairfax circuit court clerks put out original documents signed by four Founding Fathers, part of the celebration of 250 years of American independence.
Fri 10/2, 6–8 p.m. · Free · ~20 min, Charles Hamilton Houston Courthouse, King and Market Streets, Leesburg · Open to the public
Five DC Improv comics play Tally Ho, from $28.09 all in. Nelson Fuentes hosts Bill Monaghan, DLO, Amber Hendrix and Haywood Turnipseed, and the balcony two-tops have already sold out.
Fri 10/2, doors 6:30 p.m. · $28.09–$42.82 a seat, fees included · ~20 min, 19 W Market St, Leesburg · All ages
Saturday
Up to 150 show cars park on downtown Leesburg's streets on Saturday. The 38th Leesburg Car Show raises senior scholarships for Academies of Loudoun students. Spectator's Choice voting closes at 3, and awards follow at 3:30 at King and Market. Downtown streets close from 9 a.m.
Sat 10/3, 11 a.m.–4 p.m., welcome at noon; rain date Sat 10/17 · Entrance by donation, no set price · ~20 min, downtown Leesburg around King and Market Streets · All ages
Ride the ladder truck at Leesburg's fire station on Saturday, or stay for lunch at Lucketts. Both volunteer stations run tours, put their trucks on display and give fire-safety demonstrations. In Leesburg, park in the shopping center behind the station, because there's no parking at the fire or police station.
Sat 10/3; Leesburg 11 a.m.–2 p.m., Lucketts 10 a.m.–2 p.m. · Free at Lucketts; no charge posted in Leesburg · ~20 min, 61 Plaza St NE, Leesburg; ~25 min, 42429 Lucketts Rd, Leesburg · All ages
Vanish's 5K costs $44.30 through Friday and $49.64 on race day. A new course around the farm brewery, with a 1-mile race for anyone not up for three.
Sat 10/3, 10 a.m. · 5K $44.30 through Fri 10/2, $49.64 race day; 1 mile $33.63, then $38.96 · ~20 min, 42245 Black Hops Ln, Leesburg · Runners; 1-mile option for families

Sat 10/3, doors 7 p.m. · $28.09 general admission, $68.82 VIP seated, fees included · ~20 min, 19 W Market St, Leesburg · All ages

What Actually Matters

On Tuesday, supervisors take up data center rules, and you can sign up by noon to speak by phone. Car tax is due Monday.

Call by noon Tuesday to speak by phone when supervisors take up the data center grandfathering rules. The Board is scheduled to consider changes to which pending data center projects keep the old rules at its October 6 meeting. Sign up at 703-777-0200 to speak in person or by phone, or leave a message on the Board's comment line, 703-777-0115.
Tue 10/6, 4 p.m.; phone sign-up closes at noon, in-person sign-up opens 30 minutes before · Free, open meeting · ~20 min, 1 Harrison St SE, Leesburg, or by phone · Anyone
Ashburn's library takes new passport applications Friday afternoon, and staff take the photo. Bring a checkbook, because the State Department fee is check or money order only and can't go on the same check as the library's fee. It handles new passports only, so check the State Department site to see whether you can renew by mail.
Fri 10/2, noon–4 p.m. · Library fees $35 per application and $15 per photo; State Department fee for a passport book $130 age 16 and up, $100 under 16, check or money order only, one check per application · In Ashburn, 43316 Hay Rd · Anyone applying for a new passport
Car tax is due Monday, October 5, and paying late adds 10 percent. You can pay at loudoun.gov/PayMyBill, where an e-check costs nothing extra and cards carry a fee. The Treasurer's offices in Leesburg and Sterling are open 8 to 5:30 on Friday and Monday, and both have drop boxes.
Due Mon 10/5 · The amount on your bill; cards add a convenience fee · ~15 min, 46000 Center Oak Plaza, Sterling; ~20 min, 1 Harrison St SE, Leesburg · Anyone with a Loudoun personal property tax bill
