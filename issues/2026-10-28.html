Leesburg and Vienna hold their Halloween parades tonight. Students are off from Thursday through Tuesday. Thursday has trick-or-treating at the Ashburn library, Friday has Mall-O-Ween at Dulles Town Center and a sensory-friendly trick-or-treat at Segra Field, and Halloween night has a free Harvest Party with pony rides in Leesburg. Early voting ends Saturday at 5, Election Day is Tuesday, and Temple Hall Farm's pumpkin patch closes for the season the same day.

The 5-second plan
Thursday at 3 in Ashburn: Trick-or-Treat at the Library, with candy, crafts and activities.
Friday or Saturday night, about 30 minutes away: Fields of Fear at Cox Farms, $45, tickets online only, not recommended under 12.

Saturday from 4 to 9, about 20 minutes away: Cornerstone Chapel's free Harvest Party in Leesburg, with amusement rides, a petting zoo and pony rides.

Corrections
Setting the record straight
In the Oct 21 edition

We said: "Heritage India Festival's Diwali weekend fills the DTC Expo Center in Sterling on Saturday and Sunday, $5 a person, cash only at the door." (Sat 10/24, noon–9 p.m.; Sun 10/25, noon–7 p.m.) The section line also said "the Diwali festival in Sterling is $5, cash only."

What’s right: The organizer moved the festival to Saturday, November 28, noon to 9 p.m., and Sunday, November 29, noon to 7 p.m., at the same place in Sterling, because the DTC Expo Center could not get its certificate of occupancy in time. The price we printed was right: $5 a person a day, cash only at the door, and children 5 and under get in free.
