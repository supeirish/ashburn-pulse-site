Early voting opens in Ashburn on Sunday, and supervisors vote Tuesday to make their data-center pause official.

The School Board starts its review of eastern Loudoun elementary attendance zones on Thursday at 6:30. Zones decide which elementary school a child is assigned to. Thursday is an overview, and a public hearing and work session follows on October 22. Written comments go to lcpsplan@lcps.org.

Drop off household hazardous waste for free at the Leesburg Park and Ride on Saturday. The county takes oil-based paint, pesticides, pool chemicals, fluorescent bulbs and dry-cell batteries, and you show proof of Loudoun residency, like a driver's license or utility bill. Latex paint, motor oil, car batteries and electronics are turned away, and the limit is 15 gallons of liquid and 40 pounds of solids a household.
Sat 10/17, 8:30 a.m.–2:30 p.m. · Free for Loudoun residents · ~20 min, 41951 Gourley Transit Dr, Leesburg · Loudoun households

The county pays to design, install and maintain accepted projects, and Dominion and NOVEC staff answer questions. Two or more neighboring parcels on a qualifying road can apply, and applications open November 1.
Mon 10/19, 6 p.m.; second session Tue 10/27 at the Purcellville library · No charge posted · ~15 min, Cascades Library, Meeting Room A, 21030 Whitfield Pl, Potomac Falls · Neighborhoods without streetlights
Supervisors vote Tuesday, October 20, on the formal resolution for their data center and substation pause. The Board voted 7-1-1 on September 15 to start the pause, and the resolution would make it official back to that date. It would hold final votes on applications that need the Board's legislative approval while the county rewrites its data-center rules, and those applications can still go through the Planning Commission.

In the Oct 7 edition

We said: Frying Pan Farm Park's Fall Harvest Fest and Carnival (Oct. 16–18) sells an "unlimited-ride wristband," $23.25 presale and $28.25 a person per day after.

What’s right: Each wristband is good for one session only: either Friday, Oct. 16, 5–9 p.m., or Sunday, Oct. 18, 11 a.m.–6 p.m. The park lists no Saturday wristband session. The $23.25 presale price (through Friday, Oct. 16, at 5 p.m.), the $28.25 regular price and $10 parking were correct.
