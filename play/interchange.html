How to play The Interchange

    
Two words cross at their exact middle letter. You see the blank cross and one clue, and you guess only that single shared middle letter - not the whole words.

    
Example: HABIT across and CABIN down both have B in the middle - so the answer is B.

    
The day's game

    
Each day is one edition of three puzzles - Easy, then Medium, then Hard. You can score up to 15 points.
