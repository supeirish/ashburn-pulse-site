Standing strategy

Make the week's most useful local plans easy to share, save, and act on—then convert that usefulness into loyal Ashburn readers.

Operating rule

Useful before promotional

Each post should deliver the core value in the feed. The newsletter link is the next step, not the price of admission.

Weekly rule

One issue, several distinct objects

Do not compress the same caption across channels. Give each surface the form its audience can save, share, discuss, or use.

Post shapes

Reusable forms that keep the week coherent without making every post identical.

The weekend shortlist

Three to five plans with day, place, price, and the one detail that changes the decision.

The family save

A practical, screenshot-friendly set of choices for parents planning around time and cost.

The food / opening cut

What is genuinely new or worth the drive, with enough detail to act without clicking.

The game card

A low-friction invitation to play this week's local puzzle and share a score.

The 15 minutes before you post

The least glamorous task on the list is often the highest-leverage. Be useful in the conversation before asking the conversation to notice you.

Spend 15 minutes before and 20 minutes after the flagship post in the places Ashburn is already talking.
Prioritize venue, organizer, school, team, and trusted community accounts featured in the issue.
Answer practical questions first: price, parking, age fit, timing, registration, and sellout risk.
Tag only places actually included. A useful tag earns reshares; a tag cloud reads as promotion.
Do not drop a newsletter link into someone else's thread. Help first and let the profile carry the brand.
